The Protocol

Built to outlast market cycles

Every mechanic in AETHYR is designed around one principle: long-term holders should always be rewarded over short-term extraction.

Auto-deflation

1.5% of every transaction is permanently burned. Supply only goes one direction — down — tightening the float for every holder.

Real-yield staking

Rewards are paid from protocol revenue, not inflationary emissions. Stake $AETH and earn from fees the network actually generates.

Audited & locked

Two independent audits, renounced mint authority, and liquidity locked for 24 months. Trust is engineered, not promised.

On-chain governance

Every treasury allocation, fee change, and partnership is decided by token-weighted vote. The DAO is the product roadmap.

Cross-chain native

Bridge $AETH across Ethereum, Base, Solana and Arbitrum with a single canonical supply — no wrapped-asset fragmentation.

Transparent treasury

A live, public dashboard tracks every wei in the DAO treasury. No multisig black boxes — verify, don't trust.
